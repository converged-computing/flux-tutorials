How to play

  • On your turn, choose a difficulty. Thin slice moves 1 space, Deep dish 2, The Works 3.
  • The host reads the question. Your team gets one answer. The timer is a guide; the host decides when time is up.
  • Correct: move forward. Wrong: stay put, and any other team can steal for 1 space.
  • Red tiles are hazards. Land on one and you move back.
  • The first team to reach the last tile wins.

Host keys

  • 1 / 2 / 3 choose difficulty
  • Space show answer, then C correct or W wrong
